aiapps

Small tools and pages built in this repo.

  • Algorithms Quiz
    CSE 5311 midterm practice: true/false and multiple-choice questions on lectures 1–11 (through red-black trees), with explanations, spaced review and saved progress.
    /aiapps/algorithms-quiz/
  • Daily Briefing
    A 3-minute morning read: top US, India and world news with links to the full stories. Updated daily.
    /aiapps/daily-briefing/
  • Dholakpur Call
    Video calls where you become a 3D Chhota Bheem, Chutki, Raju, Kalia, Jaggu or Indumati, an open-source VRM avatar, or your own .vrm, in real time. Your expressions, head and hands carry over, so you can wave. Peer-to-peer WebRTC, and your real face never leaves the browser.
    /aiapps/dholakpur-call/
  • Flap 3D
    A glossy 3D flappy-bird-style game: tap to flap between the pipes while day turns to night.
    /aiapps/flappy-3d/
  • DFW ⇄ ORD Weekend Fares
    Daily-checked round-trip economy price for the next Friday to Sunday weekend, DFW to ORD.
    /aiapps/flight-price-tracker/
  • The Paper Plane
    A very short story, animated in three.js: a paper plane carries a letter from Dallas to Chicago.
    /aiapps/paper-plane/
  • tmux cheatsheet
    /aiapps/tmux-cheatsheet/
